## 🎨 Master Design Specifications & Philosophy (Abraham Toluwani)

1. **Minimalism:** Generous whitespace, restrained palette, zero decorative clutter.
2. **Professionalism:** Consistent grid + spacing scale, deliberate type pairing, non-template structure.
3. **Hierarchy:** Size, weight, and contrast carry importance—not color noise. `H1 Hero` > `Section Headers` > `Body`.
4. **Standout Rule:** Spend boldness in **ONE** place per section/page. Keep the rest quiet.
5. **Simplicity:** Cut anything that does not earn its place.
6. **Eye Coordination:** Deliberate visual flow (Z or F pattern), aligned grid, consistent spacing rhythm: `Hero → Work → Proof → Contact`.
