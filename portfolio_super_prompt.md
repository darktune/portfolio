## Design law
- **Minimalism** — generous whitespace, restrained palette, zero decorative clutter.
- **Professionalism** — consistent grid + spacing scale, deliberate type pairing, nothing template-generic.
- **Hierarchy** — size/weight/contrast carry importance, not color noise. H1 hero > section headers > body.
- **Standout Rule** (Abraham's own design principle) — one clear focal element per section/page. Spend boldness in ONE place, keep the rest quiet.
- **Simplicity** — cut anything that doesn't earn its place.
- **Eye coordination** — deliberate visual flow (Z or F pattern), aligned grid, consistent spacing rhythm. Guide the eye: hero → work → proof → contact.

## Avoid the AI-default look
Skip the three clichés: (1) cream bg + serif + terracotta accent, (2) black bg + single neon accent, (3) broadsheet hairline-rule columns. Earn the palette and signature element FROM the subject matter — protocol/ledger precision (D-MEX), tiered civilization systems (CivOS), atelier/fashion craft — not a default.
